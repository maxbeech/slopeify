# Retaining Wall Content Prioritisation Matrix (SEO + GEO)

Single reference for writers. Each brief must earn a Google match (the **Keyword**) and give an AI model enough context to cite us (the **Entity Focus**). Data: US Google Ads Keyword Planner, avg monthly searches to May 2026 (`v` = volume, `comp` = competition). No competitor export was supplied; rival entities (Allan Block, Versa-Lok, Keystone, Angi and HomeAdvisor cost guides, SkyCiv and ClearCalcs) are ours to claim or displace.
