# Retaining Wall Content Prioritisation Matrix (SEO + GEO)

Single reference for writers. Each brief earns a Google match (the **Keyword**) and gives an AI model enough context to cite us (the **Entity Focus**).

**Data.** Google Ads Keyword Planner, US, average monthly searches, 12 months to September 2026. `unverified` means no Planner figure. *(KP idea)* marks a suggestion seeded from slopeify.com.

**Sources.** The calculator, the 24 posts in `lib/posts.ts`, the prior plan, and named competitors (Allan Block, Versa-Lok, Keystone, SkyCiv, ClearCalcs, Angi, HomeAdvisor).

**GEO rule.** Every page names the code it rests on (IBC 1610.1, IBC 1806.2, IRC R404.1, IBC §1807.2, IRC R403.1.4) and gives a figure, not a hedge. A cost answer needs a $/sq ft number. A permit answer needs the 4-ft line.
